# Live footage: shot list

Eight short clips of the real app, filmed against a **live** instance (Muse Spark
reading, so every badge says so). The cut uses only a few seconds of each, so
calm and slow beats fast. If you can run Node, `capture/record.mjs` does all of
this for you (see README.md). Otherwise, record by hand:

**Setup for every shot**
- Browser window exactly **1920×1080** of page (full screen on a 1080p display,
  or use a window-size tool). Browser zoom **133%** (Ctrl/Cmd +) so the words read
  well on video; **150%** for the four `08…` shots, which play small in a corner.
  Hide bookmarks, notifications off.
- Record the browser window only (macOS: Cmd-Shift-5 → Record Selected Portion;
  Windows: Win-Alt-R in Xbox Game Bar; or OBS).
- Move the mouse slowly and deliberately; pause on things for 2 seconds.
- Switch who you are with the person chips at the top of the page.
- Save each clip as the file name given, into `film/footage/`
  (`.mp4`, `.mov` or `.webm` all work). Trim nothing; the assembler picks the part it needs.

| File | Who | What to do on screen | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `05-lisa-asks.mp4` | Lisa | On Home, type **Ask Daniel to pick up milk on his way home**. Send. Wait for the house's reply ("Do you want me to tell Daniel this…?"). Hold 2 s. Click **Yes**. Hold 3 s. | Section 05, 19 s |

| `07b-claims.mp4` | Daniel | Type **I'll get Mia at 3**. Send. Hold on the reply. Open **Claims**; scroll slowly to "home by three". Hold. | Section 07, last 5 s |
| `08a-wonder.mp4` | Daniel | Open **Briefing** (the house's own rules already show the double-booked soccer run). Click **Ask Muse Wonder what needs attention**. (Stop recording while it thinks if you like.) Hold 4 s on its findings and the entries it cites. | Section 08, picture-in-picture, last 4 s |
| `08b-shelf.mp4` | Lisa | Briefing → **What's on the shelf**. Choose your pantry-shelf photo (coffee, paper towels, dishwasher tablets, dish soap), click **Check it with SAM 3.1**. Hold 4 s on the outlines and counts. | Section 08, PiP, last 2.5 s |
| `08c-picture-card.mp4` | Daniel | After shot 05: open Daniel's **Inbox** and wait (reload every few seconds, up to a minute) for the picture card Muse Image draws for Lisa's milk request; hold 5 s on it. |

Tip: do the shots in this order; 05b needs 05's message, and 08c's card takes a
minute to be drawn.
