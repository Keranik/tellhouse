# Personas (fictional)

One household, one property, one shared record: Daniel and Lisa, partners, in the main house with their
children Timmy and Mia; Daniel's mother Margaret in the house's second unit; and June next door, in the
household's circle. Everyone here is invented. No real person's information is used.

## Daniel, 49
Works early shifts at the depot and is out of the house before anyone else is up. He leaves one line at the
door as he goes ("depot by nine, hardware store after, home by three"), so nobody has to text him to find out
where he is or when he'll be back. He keeps the coffee stocked and does the recycling, when he remembers.

## Lisa, 44
Works across town and is the one who usually ends up holding the whole schedule in her head: pickups, soccer,
the bill due Friday. Tellhouse is how she hands that to the house instead. Her sharing default is everyone in the household, so what she passes on can be seen by the whole house.

## Margaret, Daniel's mother
Lives in the back unit and is part of the household's day: she helps with the kids after school, plays bridge
on Tuesdays (Daniel drives, June is the backup), and tells the house what's going on the same way everyone else
does.

## Timmy and Mia
Daniel and Lisa's kids. Timmy has soccer, and it just moved to 5:30; Mia has homework most nights and a
side-gate pickup on Tuesdays. They are part of the household the house knows about, and the adults talk about
them all day ("I'll get Mia at 3"), but they don't use the house themselves: nobody under 18 does. The house
takes no message from a child and passes nothing on to one.

## June, next door
Not family and doesn't live in the house. She has a key, does the Tuesday school pickup, and reads the
household's own notes for the job ("Mia waits at the side gate"). She stands for the wider circle every
household has: the people a household includes are not only the people who sleep under its roof.
